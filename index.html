How to play

  • 1Touch & drag anywhere on the starfield. Your ship turns and thrusts toward your finger — it hovers just above your fingertip so you can always see it.
  • 2Firing is automatic. While you’re touching, the blaster fires on its own. Just steer and line up your shots.
  • 3Big meteors split in two. A big one becomes two mediums, a medium becomes two smalls, and smalls are destroyed outright.
  • 4Don’t get hit. One collision costs a ship. You have 3 ships — after a hit you respawn blinking and briefly untouchable.
  • 5Clear the sky to start the next wave: more meteors, moving a little faster each time.
